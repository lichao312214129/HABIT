HABIT Documentation#

HABIT turns medical images plus an ROI into tumour habitat maps, quantifies them (volume fractions, MSI, ITH, radiomics, and HABIT’s own habitat graph-network features), and saves a reusable .habitatmodel so new patients can be labelled without refitting.

Project repository: lichao312214129/HABIT

Warning

Research / education only. Not for clinical diagnosis.
