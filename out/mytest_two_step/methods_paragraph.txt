Habitat imaging analysis was performed with HABIT (version 1.0.1). The analysis specification 'habitat_two_step_demo_data' comprised voxel feature extraction with raw (modalities=['delay2', 'delay3', 'delay5']); supervoxelization with kmeans (n_init=5, n_supervoxels=50); habitat model fitting with kmeans (max_habitats=4, min_habitats=2, n_init=5, validation='silhouette'); habitat assignment with nearest_centroid (default parameters); habitat feature families: volume (default parameters), msi (default parameters), ith_score (default parameters); per-subject voxel feature preprocessing: winsorize, minmax; per-subject supervoxel feature preprocessing: zscore; cohort-level feature preprocessing: binning. The executed pipeline steps, in provenance order, were: recipes.habitat.two_step; habitat_assigner.nearest_centroid; habitat_assigner.nearest_centroid; feature_preprocessing.cohort; feature_preprocessing.cohort; feature_preprocessing.subject.supervoxel; feature_preprocessing.subject.supervoxel; supervoxelizer.kmeans; supervoxelizer.kmeans; feature_preprocessing.subject.voxel; feature_preprocessing.subject.voxel; voxel_feature_extractor.raw; voxel_feature_extractor.raw; subject_images; subject_images.
