Advanced
========

Each analysis stage (extract through postprocess), backends (process,
resume, GPU cap, failure isolation), embedding HABIT in your pipeline,
and exporting results with ``describe_methods``.

That can leave tiny islands of a few voxels that no reader would call a region, and that inflate ITH and graph metrics. Connected- component cleanup drops islands smaller than a size cut-off and fills those voxels with neighbouring habitat ids, so the map stays the same definition with less speck.">
