3. Precise Feature Screening
============================

Repeatability and reproducibility screening (Prior et al., 2024):
simulated image retest perturbation (noise, translation, rotation) and
ROI contour edge perturbation, evaluated across kernel radii and bin
widths with multi-panel ICC forest plots.
