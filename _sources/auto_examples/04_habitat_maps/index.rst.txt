4. Habitat Mapping
==================

From voxel features to habitat label maps: two-step habitats with SLIC supervoxels,
recipes, atomic operators, comparing clustering algorithms (k-means vs GMM
with label matching and Dice/ARI), applying a saved model, and matching
independently clustered ids.
